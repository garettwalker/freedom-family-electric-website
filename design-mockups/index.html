Freedom Family Electric — homepage concepts

Four directions for the redesign, all keeping the pine-green palette family but treating it differently. Each is a full homepage mockup built with the new photos. Click one to open it full-size, then come back and compare.

CONCEPT 1

Timber & Light

Editorial and photo-forward. Warm white ground, oversized serif headlines, full-bleed hero image, thin hairlines instead of cards. Lets the photography do the talking.
CONCEPT 2

Deep Pine

Dark, premium custom-builder feel. Deep pine-green ground with cream type and a clay call-to-action — photos glow against it. The boldest departure from the current site.
CONCEPT 3

Clean Lines

White, sharp-edged gallery direction. Zero rounded corners, hairline grid, a portfolio wall of finished rooms front and center. The most "modern and clean lines" of the four.
CONCEPT 4

Heritage Refined

The current cream/sand/pine palette, evolved — same warmth the owner likes, rebuilt with an asymmetric hero, serif accents, and stronger family-story section.
