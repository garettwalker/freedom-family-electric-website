Built on faith, family, and work that holds up.

We're a family of six in King, North Carolina. We started Freedom Family Electric on a belief that honest work, steady communication, and a job done right still matter — the same values we live by at home shape how we treat builders, homeowners, and job sites.

That belief shapes every home we wire: with care for the family who will live there, respect for the builder's timeline, and a standard of work we would want in our own house. Faith shows up in our work through service — not slogans.

When you call, you talk to the people doing the work. When we give you a timeline, we keep it. And when we close up a wall, everything behind it is something we'd be glad to live with ourselves.
